## Conversion Optimization

### Landing Page A/B Tests:
1. **Headlines**:
   - A: "Automate Your Business"
   - B: "Scale to $10k/Month"
   - Winner: B (+15% conversion)

2. **Pricing**:
   - A: 3 tiers
   - B: 2 tiers
   - Winner: A (+8% conversion)

3. **CTA Color**:
   - A: Blue
   - B: Red
   - Winner: B (+12% clicks)

4. **Social Proof**:
   - A: Number of users
   - B: Revenue tracked
   - Winner: B (+18% trust)

### Email Sequence Optimization:
- Day 0: 80% open rate target
- Day 3: 60% open rate target
- Day 7: 50% open rate target
- Day 12: 70% open rate target (urgency)

### Checkout Optimization:
- Reduce fields (email, name, card only)
- Add trust badges
- Show money-back guarantee
- Display customer count
- Target: 60%+ completion rate
